Your R&O add-in is online

This page makes the small file that tells Excel where the R&O panel lives.

Checking the add-in files…

  1. Download your manifest It already contains this website's address, so there is nothing to edit.
  2. Upload it to Excel Open RandO_Forecast_Input.xlsx in Excel for the web. Go to Home, then Add-ins, then More Add-ins (on some screens: Insert, then Add-ins). Choose My Add-ins, then Upload My Add-in, select manifest.xml and press Upload.
  3. Open the panel A button called R&O Forecast appears on the Home tab. Click it to open the panel on the right.

If Excel says the manifest is invalid, use this simpler version instead. The panel then opens straight after uploading, without a ribbon button.
